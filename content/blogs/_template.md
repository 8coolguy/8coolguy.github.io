# Optional heading for the post body

The title, year, tags, and link come from the front matter above. The blog
page shows the entry as a clickable card grouped under the year — clicking
it opens the link in a new tab.

The body below the second `---` is not shown on the listing page, but you
can keep notes or a draft here for reference.
